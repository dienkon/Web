## 2. Root-Cause Findings

1. **`pdfjs-dist` v6 (`6.5.438`) Disposal API Mismatch**:
   - `pdfjs.getDocument(...)` returns a `PDFDocumentLoadingTask` instance (which exposes `.destroy()`), while `await loadingTask.promise` resolves to a `PDFDocumentProxy` instance (which exposes `.cleanup()`, NOT `.destroy()`).
   - Calling `loadedDocInstance.destroy()` inside the `useEffect` cleanup threw `TypeError: loadedDocInstance.destroy is not a function` whenever a PDF finished loading before unmount or during React 19 StrictMode passive effect unmount checks.
2. **Loose Single-School Exam Clutter on Web**:
   - Previously, `mathvn.com` was capped at `450` entries (missing `1,000+` modern 2021–2026 exams), `includeSubfolders` defaulted to `true` inside parent folders (dumping thousands of descendant files below child folder cards), and browsing the root/subject filters displayed hundreds of pages of loose single-school exam cards instead of grouping shared topics together.
3. **Hidden / Limited User Document Supplementing**:
   - Previously, the `"Của tôi"` tab was hidden when logged out, lacked search/folder/status filters, and had no dedicated workflow to quickly append extra PDFs/DOCXs or answer keys to an existing paid or free document without re-submitting the entire creation form.

## 5. Security Boundaries

- **Ownership Enforcement**: `supplementLibraryDocument` and `createOrUpdateLibraryDocument` strictly verify `existingDoc.authorUid === actor.uid` unless `actor.role` is `admin` or `super_admin`. Non-owners cannot append files, alter pricing, or toggle visibility on another user's document.
- **Moderation Integrity**: Non-admin owners toggling visibility back from `"hidden"` respect `settings.autoApproveUserUploads` and cannot self-publish `"rejected"` documents without moderation when auto-approval is disabled.
- **Paid Secret Isolation**: `sanitizePublicDocument` continues to strip `fileUrl`, `answerUrl`, `videoUrl`, and `subFiles` from `library_documents` whenever `accessMode === "paid" && priceVnd > 0`, storing them exclusively in server-only `library_document_secrets` (while exposing `subFileCount` metadata safely).
- **SSRF Protection**: Every non-Cloudinary external URL supplied in `appendSubFiles`, `replaceSubFiles`, or `answerUrl` passes `validateUrlForSsrf` before persistence.

## 7. Remaining Risks & Prerequisites

- **External Google Drive Rate Limits on Seeded Files**: Seeded third-party Google Drive links remain subject to upstream Google Drive quota if hundreds of files are batch-downloaded simultaneously; `batchDownloadService.ts` mitigates this via bounded concurrency (`3` workers).
- **Serverless Payload Ceiling**: Direct server-proxied single-file PDF downloads (`/api/documents/pdf-proxy`) remain capped at `44 MB`; larger Ebooks automatically fall back to direct/native download links.
